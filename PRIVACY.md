# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** May 8, 2026

## Overview

Parlay is a read-only MCP service for prediction-market research. This Privacy
Policy explains what we collect, how we use it, and how we work with service
providers and data sources when you use parlay.run, the Parlay dashboard, and
Parlay MCP tools.

Parlay is not a broker, exchange, investment adviser, legal adviser, or tax
adviser. We provide research context and source links only.

## Information We Collect

### Account and Authentication Data

When you sign in, we may collect your name, email address, profile image,
authentication provider, provider account identifier, session metadata, account
status, OAuth tokens, and authorization metadata. Google sign-in may be used to
connect the website account with MCP Worker account records.

### MCP Install, Plan, and Quota Data

We may process MCP installation status, plan name, quota usage, reset dates,
enabled tools, tool names called, timestamps, request identifiers, and
account-level usage metadata. This helps us enforce plan limits, troubleshoot
issues, and protect the service from abuse.

### User-Provided Content

When you use Parlay through an MCP client, we may process the market queries,
tool arguments, selected venues, brief requests, support messages, and other
content you choose to send. Do not send sensitive personal information unless
it is necessary for your request.

We do not log your full AI-client conversation history. Parlay processes the
structured tool requests sent to the Service.

### Device, Log, and Cookie Data

We may collect IP address, user agent, browser type, device type, referring
URLs, pages viewed, error logs, and security events. We use cookies and similar
technologies for authentication, session management, preferences, fraud
prevention, and basic service analytics.

### Billing Data

When paid upgrades are enabled, Stripe may process payment method details,
billing address, transaction information, tax information, and fraud-prevention
signals. Parlay does not store full card numbers. We receive billing status,
plan status, invoice identifiers, and related account metadata needed to
provide paid plans and support refunds.

### Market Data and Source Attribution

Parlay may process market titles, market IDs, venue names, source URLs, close
dates, public market metadata, probability-like fields where available, source
retrieval timestamps, and attribution metadata. We use source attribution so
users can trace results back to the relevant venue or data source.

## What We Do Not Collect

- Full AI-client conversation history
- Personal financial account credentials
- Prediction-market venue trading credentials
- Private keys or wallet seeds
- Brokerage credentials
- Full payment card numbers

## How We Use Information

We use information to:

- create, authenticate, and secure accounts
- link website sessions with MCP Worker account records
- provide read-only MCP tools and dashboard status
- enforce Free, Pro, and Team plan limits
- respond to support requests
- process billing, invoices, and refunds when paid upgrades are enabled
- detect abuse, debug errors, and improve reliability
- preserve source attribution and explain data-source limitations
- comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms of Service

## Service Providers and Data Sources

We may share information with vendors and data sources only as needed to run
Parlay:

- **Authentication providers** such as Google OAuth and related auth tooling
  support account authentication and provider account mapping.
- **Hosting and infrastructure providers** such as Vercel and Cloudflare
  support hosting, delivery, security, caching, traffic protection, Worker
  execution, and account or quota records.
- **Database providers** such as Cloudflare D1 and Neon Postgres may host
  account, plan, quota, and dashboard records.
- **Stripe** processes paid subscriptions, invoices, checkout, billing status,
  and refunds when payment flows are enabled.
- **PMXT and data sources** may provide or help identify prediction-market
  data, source metadata, and attribution fields.
- **Prediction-market venues and public data sources** may include Polymarket,
  Kalshi, Limitless, Manifold, Opinion.trade status information, venue APIs,
  public pages, and other source-linked market data.

These providers may process information under their own privacy notices and
service terms. We do not sell personal information.

## Data Retention

We keep information for as long as needed to provide Parlay, comply with legal
obligations, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, maintain security, and
support billing records. MCP usage metadata and logs may be retained for abuse
prevention, debugging, quota enforcement, and support.

Authentication tokens are retained for as long as your account is active or as
needed to maintain authorized MCP access. Tool invocation logs may be retained
for up to 90 days for operational and security purposes, then deleted or
anonymized unless longer retention is required for security, fraud prevention,
billing, dispute resolution, or legal compliance.

## Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards, including access
controls, TLS in transit, provider-managed infrastructure controls, and secret
management. No internet service is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee
absolute security.

## Your Choices

You can:

- stop using Parlay at any time
- revoke OAuth authorization through your AI client's connector settings
- manage browser cookies through your browser settings
- contact us to request access, correction, deletion, or export of personal
  information where applicable
- request account help at support@parlay.run

Some information may be retained where required for security, fraud prevention,
billing, dispute resolution, or legal compliance.

## International Processing

Parlay and its service providers may process information in the United States
and other locations where they operate. By using Parlay, you understand that
information may be transferred to and processed in those locations.

## Children

Parlay is not directed to children under 18. Do not use Parlay if you are under
18 or below the age required to use prediction-market research tools in your
jurisdiction.

## Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. The updated policy will
be posted with a new effective date. Continued use after changes constitutes
acceptance of the updated policy.
